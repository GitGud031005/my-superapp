Small skewed label for status or category. Not interactive.

```jsx
<Tag tone="red">Wanted</Tag> <Tag tone="yellow">New</Tag> <Tag tone="black">Cleared</Tag>
```

`tone="sticker"` is the emphasis style: yellow slab with a black then white outline and a hard offset. Use it once per view for something that just happened ("Rank up!"), not as a general label.

```jsx
<Tag tone="sticker">Rank up!</Tag>
```
