The cut-out ransom-note lettering from the Phantom Thieves calling cards. Each letter is its own tile with a random typeface, skin, tilt and cut edge, seeded so it is repeatable. Use it for titles and short messages, never for paragraphs; keep body copy in the body font.

```jsx
<RansomTitle as="h1" text="Take your heart" skin="logo" size={72} />
<div style={{ width: 600, height: 160 }}><RansomTitle text="You hoard unfinished projects" fit maxSize={64} /></div>
```
